Markers shown are literature-inspired research topics, not measured cytokine levels or inferred clinical biomarkers.

04 / Ingredient-matching concept

The original AITEKIT prototypes used predefined associations between selected microorganisms, skin conditions and cosmetic ingredients. This panel illustrates the concept, not a scientifically validated matching algorithm.

No treatment or prescription recommendations are generated. Ingredient suitability requires evidence, safety assessment, formulation compatibility and regulatory review.
